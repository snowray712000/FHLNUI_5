### 創1:4

Case:
"\n * that.\n # 10,12,18,25,31; Ec 2:13; 11:7|\n * the light from the darkness.  Heb. between the light and\n   between the darkness.\n\n"

- 歸納
  - **新規則出現**
  - 這是第一次出現「關鍵字」卻沒有「交互參照」的情況。
  - 請看對應的 KJV 譯文
    - And God called the light Day, and the darkness he called Night. And the evening and the morning were the first day. \<RF>And the evening...: Heb. And the evening was, and the morning was etc.\<Rf>\<CM>
  - 再看 TSK 的內容
    - * the light from the darkness.  Heb. between the light and between the darkness.
  - 可發現
    - KJV 中，會使用 ...: 那個「...」若展開，就是 KJV 內文的「And the evening and the morning were the first day.」
    - KJV 的 RF 中，意思是，Heb 原文的意思更接近是「And the evening was, and the morning was etc.」
    - TSK 中，也是加上註腳，說明「the light from the darkness」的原文是「between the light and between the darkness」

- 幾大類
  - 章節摘要
  - 關鍵字/句子，的註腳，和 交互參照。
  - 交互參照 (for 某節，不是針對某個關鍵字/句子
- 特殊格式
  - [ ... ,] 原文
  - \<FB> See definition 02053 \<Fb>
